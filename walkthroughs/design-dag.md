# Design with Sources, Transforms & Sinks

Build your pipeline graphically by connecting nodes together on the canvas.

### Visual Components

- **Sources**: Read data from BigQuery, Google Cloud Pub/Sub, Apache Kafka, JDBC databases, or files.
- **Transforms**: Process, filter, map, aggregate, flatten, or window streaming/batch data.
- **Sinks**: Write results to BigQuery, Pub/Sub, Kafka, JDBC, or cloud storage.

### Editing Properties

Click any node on the canvas to open its **Properties Panel** on the right side. Required fields are clearly highlighted, with built-in Apache Beam documentation tooltips on every option.
